
the question arises. how do I get what I want / how do I maximize my reward?

Assuming BIg World Hypothesis / a general inability for an agent to perfectly model the world due to complexity

the two limits of reward maximization include:
(i) increasing control over your environment, such that my locus of control increases and there are less things that surprise me
(ii) changing your reward function such that your environment can maximize it

My contention is that in a big world hypothesis (https://oaklab.ai/posts/the-big-world-hypothesis), you're best off engaging in (ii) as a meta-strategy. 

that's not to say (i) isn't viable for *short-term* reward maximization. *long-term* reward maximization is achieved by finding meta-strategies that allow you to edit your reward function directly. 

the danger with (ii) is that you risk "wire-heading" or reward hacking yourself into some kind of reward function that drives you to extremely high negative rewards in your prior reward function. This is maximizing adaptation. 

thus the meta-strategy has to find take actions that result in long-term reward gain that ***also*** increase control over your own reward function. I think this can be done via skillful means, as a human, that is best achieved imo at a meditative / spiritual level
