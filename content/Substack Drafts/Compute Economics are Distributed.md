Appendix idea:
There are two counteracting forces:
- Cost optimization across every component of the supply chain. Hayekian, Lockean, Hansonian
- Increasing compounding returns given to the best capital allocators. Centralized singleton as optimizer (bostrom, dario, kurzweil)

These oscillate, counteract, and result in the construction of coasean boundaries.

The hayekian future is one where the chips and their location alone determine economics. Diffusion of chips and sensor geographically, software margins compress to zero, etc.

The singleton future is one where innovations on the software layer result in better utilization of compute. Consolidation and capture of compute. OpenAI / Anthropic.
