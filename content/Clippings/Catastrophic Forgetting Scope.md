# Project Scope: Catastrophic Forgetting via Sequential Full Fine-Tuning

## Objective

Sequentially full-fine-tune an open-weights 8B model on two disjoint-domain tasks to induce catastrophic forgetting, quantified via task-score and perplexity deltas. Primary artifact: a forgetting curve of Task-A score over Task-B steps.

## Background Reading (in order)

1. McCloskey 1989, _Catastrophic Interference in Connectionist Networks: The Sequential Learning Problem_ — sequential training overwrites earlier associations.
2. Goodfellow 2013, _An Empirical Investigation of Catastrophic Forgetting in Gradient-Based Neural Networks_ — forgetting severity depends on task pairing and training choices.
3. Luo 2023, _An Empirical Study of Catastrophic Forgetting in Large Language Models During Continual Fine-tuning_ — LLMs forget knowledge and reasoning; worsens with scale.
4. Biderman 2024, _LoRA Learns Less and Forgets Less_ — full fine-tuning maximizes both learning and forgetting.
5. Shi 2024, _Continual Learning of Large Language Models: A Comprehensive Survey_ — field map: settings, methods, evaluation.
6. Mangrulkar 2023, _Fine-tuning Llama 2 70B using PyTorch FSDP_ — practical multi-GPU FSDP fine-tuning walkthrough.

## Model & Infra

- Model: meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B (base), bf16.
- Compute: one 8× A100 80GB node; 1 TB NVMe scratch.
- Training: PyTorch 2.x + Hugging Face Transformers Trainer via Accelerate; FSDP full-shard with activation checkpointing.
- Eval: lm-evaluation-harness (GSM8K, MMLU); bigcode-evaluation-harness (HumanEval).

## Task Sequence & Data

- Task A (math): GSM8K train, 7,473 examples; hold out 500 (seed 42) for perplexity; fine-tune on 6,973.
- Task B (code): CodeAlpaca-20k, 20,022 instruction–completion pairs.
- Max sequence 2,048; loss on completion tokens only.

## Training Protocol

Identical recipe per task: AdamW, peak LR 2e-5, cosine decay, 3% warmup, grad-clip 1.0, weight decay 0, global batch 128 (8 GPUs × micro-batch 4 × grad-accum 4), 3 epochs. Task A ≈165 steps; Task B ≈470 steps from post-A final weights. Checkpoint (weights-only, ~16 GB) every 25 optimizer steps, both tasks.

1. Baseline eval: GSM8K, MMLU, held-out perplexity.
2. Fine-tune Task A. Eval GSM8K at every checkpoint; post-A peak = best checkpoint. Post-A eval adds MMLU.
3. Fine-tune Task B. Eval GSM8K + held-out perplexity at every checkpoint — the forgetting curve.
4. Post-B final eval: GSM8K, HumanEval, MMLU, held-out perplexity.

## Evaluation & Metrics

- GSM8K test: 1,319 items, 0-shot, strict exact-match.
- HumanEval: 164 problems, greedy pass@1.
- MMLU: full test set, 14,042 questions, 5-shot.
- Perplexity: 500 held-out GSM8K solutions, completion tokens.
- Forgetting F = GSM8K(post-A peak) − GSM8K(post-B final), percentage points.
- ΔPPL = PPL(post-B final) − PPL(post-A peak).

## Deliverables

1. Forgetting-curve plot: GSM8K accuracy and held-out PPL vs Task-B step; post-A peak marked.
2. Results table: baseline / post-A peak / post-B final × all four metrics.
3. ≤1 page written observations.

## Out of Scope

Mitigation methods (replay, EWC, regularization, PEFT/LoRA); multiple seeds; cross-model comparisons; hyperparameter sweeps.
